Account Statement Reports

Clear Partner Statements in One Workspace

Open one full-width statement screen, switch between Customer and Vendor, adjust the filters directly, review transaction activity and export a professional statement without using separate wizards.

Customer & Vendor in One Screen

Use the same Account Statements workspace for receivable and payable partner activity, with a simple Customer / Vendor selector.

Editable Filters

Filter by partner, date range, company, journals, accounts, posted state and opening-balance option directly from the report page.

Opening & Running Balance

Track the opening balance and follow the running balance transaction by transaction until the final statement balance.

Real-time Totals

Opening balance, total debit, total credit and final balance remain visible above the transaction table for quick review.

Detailed Transaction History

Review transaction date, journal entry, reference, due date, debit, credit and running balance in a compact accounting-style table.

Clickable Journal Entries

Open the related accounting entry directly from the statement line when you need to inspect the original transaction.

Professional PDF Statement

Print a clean customer or vendor statement with company information, statement period, transaction details and totals.

Excel Export with Totals

Export the transaction history to Excel with debit, credit and final-balance totals included at the bottom of the report.
